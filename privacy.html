Privacy policy

short-gen is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

The app only requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It does not collect, store, share or sell any data from other users. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are never shared with third parties.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time via Google account permissions.
